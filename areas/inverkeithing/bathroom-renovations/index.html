Bathroom Renovations for Inverkeithing’s tenement flats

Around the historic High Street and the harbour, Inverkeithing has old stone buildings and flats, with post-war and newer homes on the slopes above the town.

Tenement bathrooms are often small, with awkward soil stacks and old floors. We plan the layout around what the building allows, so the finished room works with the space instead of fighting it.

Near the coast, good ventilation and quality fittings make a real difference to how long a bathroom stays looking new. We’ll talk you through materials that last.
