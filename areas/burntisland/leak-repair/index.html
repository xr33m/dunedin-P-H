Leak Detection & Repair in Burntisland

What Burntisland homes need

Between the Links, the beach and the High Street, Burntisland has Victorian terraces, traditional stone flats and newer homes on the edges of town.

Older homes near the coast see more corroded fittings and weeping joints. We’ll find which joint is actually leaking and replace what needs replacing — not the whole run.

Leaks in tenements often start in one flat and show up in another. We'll trace it up the building if needed — and if it's coming from a neighbour's pipes, we'll tell you straight so you can sort it with them.

What’s included

  • Tracing hidden and slow leaks
  • Leaks under baths, showers and sinks
  • Ceiling and wall damp from pipework
  • Toilet and cistern leaks
  • Leaking valves, joints and fittings
  • A clear explanation of what caused it

Can you come out for a slow leak in Burntisland, not just emergencies?

Yes. Slow leaks are worth fixing before they become emergencies. Most leak investigations in Burntisland are booked same-day or next-day.

Do you cover all of Burntisland?

Yes — KY3 and the streets around the Links, the beach and the High Street. We also cover Kirkcaldy, Dalgety Bay, Inverkeithing and Rosyth.

Will you need to lift floors or open walls?

Only if there’s no other way to reach the leak — and we’ll explain why before we do. Where we open something up, we make good access so it’s easy to reach next time.
