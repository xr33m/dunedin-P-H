General Plumbing & Heating in Newington

General Plumbing & Heating for Newington’s tenement flats

From Clerk Street and Newington Road to the Victorian villas around Mayfield and the edge of the Grange, Newington mixes tenement flats — many of them let — with larger family homes.

Tenement plumbing has usually been altered by every owner for a century. We sort out what’s there properly, and tell you honestly what needs doing now and what can wait.

Bigger period houses come with bigger systems — several bathrooms, long pipe runs and the odd mystery valve. We’re happy to make sense of it for you.
