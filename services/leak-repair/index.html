Questions

Leak Detection & Repair in Edinburgh: Your Questions Answered

How do you find a hidden leak?

We start with where the water is showing and work back along the pipework, waste and seals, checking each in turn. Most leaks are found without opening up walls or floors.

The leak's coming from the flat above — can you still help?

Yes. We'll confirm where it's coming from and, if it's not yours, isolate what we can and give you the facts to take to your neighbour or factor.

Will you need to lift floors or open walls?

Only if there’s no other way to reach the leak — and we’ll explain why before we do. Where we open something up, we make good access so it’s easy to reach next time.
