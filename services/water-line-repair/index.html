When a water mains pipe starts to degrade, the biggest source of stress is usually the disruption — water off, neighbours affected, and pressure to get it sorted fast. We give straightforward advice on the options, loop in anyone else affected, and get the work done quickly.

This is exactly the kind of job we're called out for regularly across Edinburgh's older tenement stock, where supply pipes are often original and shared between flats.

Questions

What people ask before booking

My mains pipe is shared with my neighbours — can you still help?

Yes — this is one of the jobs we handle most. We'll talk everyone affected through the options and the cost split before any work starts.

Is this always an emergency job?

Not always. A slowly degrading pipe can often be scheduled; a sudden leak gets a same-day response.

How much disruption should I expect?

We plan for minimum disruption and keep you updated throughout — no surprise digging or extended water outages without warning.

Don't wait on this

That drip won't still be a drip by morning.

A small leak left overnight is a soaked ceiling by breakfast — and a far bigger bill than calling now. We're on call 24 hours a day, every day, and a real person answers.
