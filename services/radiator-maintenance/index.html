Radiator Maintenance & Installation

A radiator that's warm at the bottom and stone cold at the top isn't "just old" — it's a system that needs attention, not necessarily a new radiator. We diagnose the actual cause before quoting for one, and keep your heating balanced and efficient whether that's a single room or the whole house.

Questions

What people ask before booking

My radiator is cold at the top but warm at the bottom — is that serious?

Usually it's trapped air or a system that needs balancing, not a sign you need a new radiator. We diagnose it properly rather than defaulting to a replacement quote.

Do you only do full house re-fits?

No — we take on single radiator repairs and bleeds just as readily as whole-house installations.

How much does a call-out cost?

You'll know the price before we start, whether it's a five-minute bleed or a full system powerflush.

Don't wait on this

That drip won't still be a drip by morning.

A small leak left overnight is a soaked ceiling by breakfast — and a far bigger bill than calling now. We're on call 24 hours a day, every day, and a real person answers.
