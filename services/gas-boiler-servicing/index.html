Questions

What people ask before booking

How often should my boiler actually be serviced?

Once a year is standard for most manufacturers' warranties — and it's usually what catches small issues before they become breakdowns.

Will you try to sell me a new boiler I don't need?

No. If a repair is the right call, we say so. We only recommend replacement when it genuinely is the better option for you.

What's included in a service?

A full safety and efficiency check of the whole system — not just a five-minute look and a sticker.
