Lead Pipe Replacement

If your building is older than the 1970s, there's a real chance water still reaches your taps through the same lead pipe that was there a century ago. We identify it, explain the actual options honestly — no scare tactics, no upsell — and replace it with safe, modern pipework with minimum disruption to your building.

  • Compliant, modern pipework fitted throughout

Lead pipework was standard in Scottish tenement and period-property plumbing for decades, and a lot of it is still in service today. It's not always an emergency, but degrading lead supply pipes are worth dealing with properly rather than patching repeatedly.

We've handled exactly this kind of job before — including a shared mains pipe serving multiple flats in a tenement close, where the priority was clear communication with everyone affected and getting the water back on with minimum disruption.

Questions

What people ask before booking

How do I know if I actually have lead pipes?

If your property was built before the 1970s and nothing's been upgraded since, there's a good chance at least part of the supply is lead. We check on-site and tell you honestly — you won't be sold a replacement you don't need.

Will this mean the water gets shut off for my whole building?

Sometimes, briefly — especially on shared tenement supplies. We coordinate with any neighbours affected in advance and keep the water-off time as short as possible.

What does it cost?

It depends on how much pipework needs replacing and how accessible it is. We'll give you a clear price before any work starts — not an estimate that changes halfway through.
