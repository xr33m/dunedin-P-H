Our Story: An Edinburgh Plumber Built on Repeat Customers

At Dunedin Plumbing & Heating, we fix leaks, refit bathrooms and keep heating running across Edinburgh, the Lothians and Fife. Every job gets the same thing: a named plumber who turns up, explains the problem straight, and agrees the price before a single tool comes out.

We didn’t grow by advertising — we grew because people who used us once called us again, for the next tap, the next radiator, the next renovation. Landlords trust us with their properties, families call us at 11pm when a pipe bursts, and neighbours pass our number along. That’s the whole model, and we protect it on every job.
