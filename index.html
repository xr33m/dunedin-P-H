About Dunedin Plumbing & Heating

We didn't grow by advertising. We grew by turning up.

Dunedin Plumbing & Heating has never relied on big marketing spend — it grew because people who used us once called us again, for the next tap, the next radiator, the next renovation. Turn up when we say we will, explain the options honestly, and price the work once, upfront.

One job that sums us up: a shared mains pipe failing in a tenement close, serving several flats at once. We walked every household through the options together, agreed a fair cost everyone could see, and got the water back on with as little disruption as possible.
