About Dunedin Plumbing & Heating

We didn't grow by advertising. We grew by turning up.

Dunedin Plumbing & Heating has never relied on big marketing spend — it grew because people who used us once called us again, for the next tap, the next radiator, the next renovation. That's the whole model: turn up when we say we will, explain the options honestly, and price the work once, upfront.

One of the jobs that best sums us up wasn't a big one — it was a shared mains pipe degrading in a tenement close, serving several flats at once. Instead of picking a price and hoping nobody asked questions, we walked every household through the options together, agreed a fair cost everyone could see, and got the water back on with as little disruption as possible. That's the standard, whether it's one leaking tap or a full renovation: explain it straight, price it honestly, finish what we start.
